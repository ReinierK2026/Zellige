School Finance Health Check

Ten questions across planning, governance, controls, liquidity and risk, based on published guidance from school accreditors, finance bodies and charity regulators. Your score builds as you go.

PlanningGFOA · NEASC · NBOA
01 We have a board-approved financial plan covering at least five years.
02 Our budget is built from enrolment and staffing drivers, not last year plus a percentage.
GovernanceCharity Commission CC8 · CC19
03 Our finance committee receives a clear monthly or termly pack it can challenge.
04 We have written reserve and investment policies the board has approved and reviews yearly.
ControlsISA / AU-C 265 · CC8
05 Our last audit reported no significant deficiencies or material weaknesses in internal control.
06 Delegations of authority and approval limits are documented and followed.
LiquidityNBOA · NORI · GFOA
07 We hold at least three months (25%) of annual operating expenses in unrestricted cash or reserves.
08 We model how currency movements affect salaries, fees and suppliers.
RiskCharity Commission CC26
09 We maintain a risk register that the board reviews at least annually.
10 We have tested what an enrolment drop of at least 10% would do to our finances.

Questions are based on guidance from NEASC accreditation standards, NBOA, GFOA and the UK Charity Commission (reserves, internal controls and risk management). Benchmarks are reference points, not rules. Every school’s right level depends on its own risks.
