What is a fractional CFO?

A senior finance leader, for the days you need one

A fractional CFO is an experienced chief financial officer who joins your leadership team part-time, on an ongoing arrangement, rather than as a full-time employee.

You get what a CFO brings: multi-year planning, clear reporting to the board, cash and risk oversight, and someone accountable for the numbers. You pay for a few days a month instead of a full salary, housing and an international search.

Full-time hire

Director of Finance or CFO

Employed every day. Full salary and benefits, often housing and relocation, and a search that can take months. Right when the school needs senior finance leadership every day.

This service

Fractional CFO

Part of your leadership team a few days a month. Owns planning and board reporting and works through your business office. Fixed monthly fee and no relocation.

One-off

Consultant or auditor

Engaged for a project or an independent opinion. Delivers a report and leaves. Doesn’t sit with your leadership or own the plan afterwards.

Is this right for your school?

A good fit, and when it isn’t

A good fit if…

✓You have roughly 300–1,500 students and a business manager, but no CFO-level leader.
✓Your head of school is acting as the de facto CFO.
✓Your finance committee wants clearer reporting and a multi-year plan.
✓A campus project, a new finance system or an accreditation visit is coming up.

Probably not the right fit if…

–You already have a full-time CFO and finance team. A one-off Independent Review may suit you better.
–Your finance leader has just left and you need someone in the office. Interim cover is designed for that.
–You’re looking for day-to-day bookkeeping or payroll processing.
The process

From first conversation to a stronger finance office

Step 1Diagnose

A confidential 45-minute call with your head or board chair. We talk through your finances, your team and what the school or board needs. There’s no obligation.

Step 2Scope

A written scope covering what we’ll own, what your business office keeps, how many days a month, and a fixed monthly fee.

Step 3Embed

We review the numbers, controls and policies, rebuild the board pack, and start the five-year plan. Your finance committee sees the first new pack within the quarter.

Step 4Run, coach, hand over

A steady monthly rhythm, coaching for your business manager, and documented processes. The goal is that you need us less over time.

Questions

What heads and boards usually ask

How is this different from hiring a Director of Finance?

You get senior strategic finance for the days you need it, without a full-time salary, a housing and relocation package or a long search. Many schools use a fractional arrangement until they’re ready to hire, and we can help you recruit and hand over.

Will this undermine our business manager?

No. The business manager keeps running operations. We add the strategic layer and coach them, so the capability stays in the school.

How much of this is remote?

Most of it. We’re on-site for key board and finance-committee meetings and budget workshops. Travel is agreed in the scope.

What happens after six months?

We review together. You can continue, reduce the days, or move to a permanent hire with a structured handover.
