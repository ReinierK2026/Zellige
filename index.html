ABOUT ZELLIGE

A school year is a mosaic of small decisions.

Zellige is the craft of setting thousands of hand-cut tiles until a picture appears. School finance works the same way: one fee decision, one hire, one currency call at a time, until they add up to the year the board sees. We keep an eye on the whole picture while you place the pieces.

Kids before the school gateA father and his son, laughingChasing a kite after class

Zellige is a finance practice built for one kind of client: independent international schools in Asia that need CFO-level judgement but cannot justify, or cannot currently fill, a full-time post.

We work the way a good finance director does. We sit inside your business office rather than around it, take the budget cycle and the board pack as our own, and hand back a stronger function than we found. The practice is led by a chartered accountant (CA(SA)) and MBA who trained in audit at KPMG in the UK and South Africa, holds the ASBO International School Finance Officer certification, is GRI-certified for sustainability reporting, and has spent the past three years working inside international-school finance and risk in Asia.
