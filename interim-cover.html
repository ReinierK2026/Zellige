Interim cover · In detail

Keep the finance office steady while you recruit.

When your Director of Finance or Business Manager leaves, the budget cycle, the audit and the board don’t wait. Interim cover keeps them on track until your permanent hire arrives, then hands over cleanly.

Typical length3–9 months
IntensityPart-time to near full-time
WhereOn-site and remote, agreed up front
Ends withA documented handover
When schools call

The situations interim cover is built for

A resignation mid-budget cycle

Next year’s budget still needs to reach the board on time.

A long international search

Senior finance hires abroad can take months to recruit and relocate.

An audit or accreditation visit

An auditor or visiting team is due, and nobody senior owns the preparation.

A leadership transition

A new head arrives and needs a clear, trustworthy financial picture.

Working with your recruitment

Cover while you search, not instead of it

Interim cover works alongside your search, whether you run it yourself or through a search firm. We can help define what the role actually needs, sit on the interview panel for technical finance questions, and make sure your new hire inherits an organised office rather than a backlog.

Questions
How quickly can you start?

Remote cover can usually begin soon after the scope is agreed. On-site work depends on your school’s location and the right to work there, which we confirm together before starting.

How is it priced?

A fixed monthly fee based on the days needed, agreed in writing before we start. Travel is agreed separately.

What if the search takes longer than planned?

We extend month by month. You’re never locked into a long commitment because a search overran.

A confidential 45-minute call. If there’s a fit, you’ll get a written plan and fee.
