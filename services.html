Everything else a school CFO gets asked to do.

Beyond fractional CFO support, interim cover and the Independent Review, these are the projects schools most often need senior finance help with. Each can be a standalone project with a fixed fee, or added to an ongoing arrangement.

01 · Governance & risk

Stronger oversight for heads and boards

Enterprise risk management

Build or refresh your risk register, agree risk appetite with the board, and set up reporting that the board actually uses.

Project or ongoing

Finance policy review

Reserves, investment, procurement, delegations of authority, fraud and whistleblowing. Benchmarked, rewritten and ready for board approval.

Project

Internal controls review

A walk-through of how money moves through the school, with the gaps ranked by risk and a practical fix list.

Project

Board and committee development

Finance training for trustees, committee terms of reference, and a reporting calendar that fits your board cycle.

Workshop or project
02 · Commercial & contracts

Better terms, fewer surprises

Contract review

The financial and commercial terms in supplier, catering, transport, lease and service agreements, reviewed alongside your legal counsel.

Per contract

Procurement and tenders

Tender design, bid evaluation and supplier negotiation for major contracts, from IT to construction.

Project

Fee strategy and pricing

Fee setting, sibling and staff discounts, scholarships and bursaries, modelled against enrolment and affordability.

Project

Staff cost and benefits modelling

Salary scales, housing and allowances, end-of-service obligations and the long-run cost of your staffing model.

Project
03 · Strategy & capital

Big decisions, properly modelled

Enrolment and scenario modelling

Driver-based models that show the board what happens under a fall in enrolment, a currency shock or a fee freeze.

Project

Campus and capital projects

Business cases, funding options, lender discussions and cost control through construction.

Project or ongoing

Treasury, currency and banking

Cash forecasting, currency exposure, banking relationships and a board-approved investment policy.

Project or ongoing

Due diligence and school groups

Financial due diligence for investors, mergers and new campuses, and integration into a group finance function.

Project

A business office that runs itself

Finance system selection

Requirements, vendor shortlisting and implementation oversight for a new finance or ERP system.

Project

Business office review

Structure, roles, workload and processes, with a plan to close gaps and grow your own people.

Project

Audit readiness and auditor tender

Accreditation finance standards

Preparing the finance and governance evidence that accreditors such as CIS, NEASC and WASC ask for.

Project

Sustainability reporting
